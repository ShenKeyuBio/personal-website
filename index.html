About

About

I am a developmental biology researcher currently working in the Naomi Moris Lab at the Francis Crick Institute. I will begin my doctoral research in the Aztekin Lab at the Friedrich Miescher Laboratory of the Max Planck Society in Tübingen. My research interests include embryonic patterning, stem-cell-based developmental models, morphogen signalling, and limb development and regeneration.

Research

Research

Developmental patterning

How cells interpret spatial and temporal signals to coordinate lineage decisions and tissue patterning during early development.

Stem-cell-based developmental models

Using human stem-cell-based models, including gastruloids and related systems, to study axial development and neuromesodermal progenitors.

Limb development and regeneration

Exploring how signalling centres such as the AER and ZPA organize limb development, patterning, and regenerative responses across species.
